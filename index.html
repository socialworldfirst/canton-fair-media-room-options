Twenty shortlisted rooms, halls, decks and rooftops for interviews and filmed conversations around the Canton Fair Complex in Pazhou. Ranked by how close they sit to the halls, with what each one gives you on camera and what to check before you commit.

How to read this. Distance is straight-line from the China Import and Export Fair Complex on Yuejiang Zhong Road, so treat it as a sort order rather than a walking time. Everything in the first four groups is on Pazhou island. The Canton Tower corridor and Zhujiang New Town options sit across the river: they buy you the landmark backdrop and cost you twenty minutes each way in fair-week traffic. Nothing here is booked. Canton Fair Complex · Official Press & Media Centre

广交会展馆 新闻中心（A区）

Address
Area A, China Import & Export Fair Complex, 380 Yuejiang Zhong Rd, Haizhu
Getting there
0 min, inside the venue
Backdrop
Canton Fair branding wall, hall floor through glass

Why it works. The organiser's own press centre. Cheapest credibility in the building: accredited media are already there, and a guest walks in off the hall floor with a badge. Best for a same-day sit-down with a trade journalist.

Watch out. Controlled by China Foreign Trade Centre, so you get a slot, not a set. No branding freedom, no exclusivity, and no Canton Tower in shot.

  • Free/accredited
  • Zero travel
  • No branding control

Langham Place, Guangzhou

广州南丰朗豪酒店

Address
638 Xingang East Rd, Pazhou, Haizhu
Getting there
approx. 0.4 km · 5 min walk
Backdrop
Rooftop bar at night; Great Hall for a dressed set

Why it works. Directly opposite the complex, on top of the Nan Fung exhibition podium. The rooftop bar is the single easiest 'drinks-and-camera' set-up in Pazhou: you can run panel interviews by day in a function room and move the same guests upstairs for the evening piece.

Watch out. Rooftop is weather-dependent and shared with hotel guests unless you buy it out. Get the buy-out price before you build the run-of-show.

  • 5 min walk
  • Rooftop bar
  • Function rooms

Nan Fung International Convention & Exhibition Centre (NICEC)

南丰国际会展中心

Address
630–638 Xingang East Rd, next to Xingang Dong station Exit F
Getting there
approx. 0.4 km · 5 min walk
Backdrop
Blank-canvas hall: build the set you want

Why it works. Four halls, 25,000 sqm, in the same podium as Langham Place. Take a corner of a hall and build a proper studio: black drape, LED wall, two sets, holding room. This is the option if you want the media room to look like WorldFirst, not like a hotel.

Watch out. Build cost and rigging time. You are paying for square metres and a crew, not a ready room. Needs a 2-day bump-in.

  • Build your own set
  • 5 min walk
  • Metro Exit F

琶洲码头 珠江岸边

Address
Yuejiang Zhong Rd riverfront, north of the complex
Getting there
approx. 0.5 km · 7 min walk
Backdrop
Open Pearl River, ferries, Canton Tower up-river at dusk

Why it works. Free, outdoors, and two minutes from Area A. The strongest cheap option for a walk-and-talk or a stand-up piece with real river traffic behind the guest. Golden hour here is the best-value shot in Pazhou.

Before you pick one

Three questions decide this list. Does the guest need the Canton Fair halls visibly behind them, or the Canton Tower skyline? Is the room a working media room running all day, or a one-hour evening set? And who is hosting the space, because the organiser, a hotel, a broadcaster and a public riverside each need a different conversation and a different lead time.

If the story is the fair

  • Official Press Centre, Area A
  • Area D conference rooms
  • Nan Fung exhibition halls

If the story is the skyline

  • Guangzhou International Media Port
  • Park Hyatt Roof Bar
  • Haixin Bridge riverfront

Lowest risk, fastest yes

  • Shangri-La Hotel, Guangzhou
  • InterContinental Guangzhou Exhibition Center
  • Langham Place, Guangzhou

Still to confirm

  • Fair-week availability at every hotel
  • Filming permits for public riverside sites
  • GRT studio access and a media partner
